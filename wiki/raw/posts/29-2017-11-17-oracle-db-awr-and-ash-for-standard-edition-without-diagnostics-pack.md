Panorama is my free performance analysis tool for Oracle-DB.

From now it offers it's own database workload recording 'Panorama-Sampler' as alternative to use of Oracle's origin AWR and ASH data.

This enables you to use Panorama's functions for evaluation of historic workload info:

- with any database edition including Standard Edition

- without licensing of Diagnostics Pack for Enterprise Edition

In addition to Oracle's AWR Panorama-Sampler may also record historic information for:

- Size evolution of tablespace objects

- Usage of DB-cache by objects

- Detailed info for blocking lock scenarios

You may find more about Panorama and it's sampling feature including download link at:

- http://rammpeter.github.io/panorama_sampler.html
